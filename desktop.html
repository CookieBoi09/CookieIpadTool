Open Lint

  1. Unzip the download and drag Lint.app into Applications.
  2. Open it. This free build is locally signed, but not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway for Lint, then confirm. Don’t disable Gatekeeper globally.
  3. Export your flashcards and notes from the website, then restore each backup in the app. The app has its own local library; it does not automatically sync with the website.

Draw with the trackpad

  1. Create a note and move the cursor over its drawing page. The dotted rectangle follows the cursor.
  2. Click the page to leave the text editor. Hold Space to lock the rectangle, then place one finger on the trackpad.
  3. Your finger’s absolute position maps into the rectangle. Lift your finger between strokes. Release Space to reposition or scroll normally.

Choose Pen or Highlighter, ink color, and width. If the app receives no touch input, lift and replace your finger while Space is held, or turn trackpad positioning off to use regular cursor drawing.

Trackpad input uses public AppKit NSTouch APIs only while Lint is focused. No global input monitoring, private Apple frameworks, or browser extension is required. Physical trackpad behavior still needs device validation.
